## Outcome and research scope

1. Maximize the chance of winning the DOE GEMS challenge with a **new, genuinely unique and high-scoring** GeoTIFF for previously unmapped faults and hidden geothermal systems. Never submit an old file under a new name. Negative findings are legitimate deliverables; distinguish research downloads from a candidate explicitly approved for competition.
2. Review GEMSDOE24, 25, 27, 28, 30, 31, 32, 33 and 36. Publish an executive summary and simple GitHub Pages site with an early, unambiguous download/submission status, an explanatory submission subpage, an auditable table for each site, official sources, and data/limitations. Keep the feed useful and current without prohibited automation.
3. Investigate the user-reported H33 score of 0.2778 and whether a unique approach can beat it and the leaderboard. A historical score-to-file association requires an organizer receipt; otherwise label it a claim. Do not substitute an unsupported leaderboard projection for a measured score.
4. Preregister 3–5 ranked geological hypotheses: name the data layers and transformations, geological mechanism, likely non-fault mimics, differences from previous implementations, expected benefits/costs, and accessible official/public/licensed data. Test the top lane with a spatial holdout before using a slot. Use only legitimate access and do not solicit secrets or bypass login.

## Experiment protocol and stop conditions

- Work within the specified single-method lane. Before placement and again on final dots, comprehensively compare against **every** applicable registry raster. Stop and log a duplicate if rank correlation exceeds 0.90 **or** more than 70% of candidate dots are within three pixels of any one registry raster. Before placement, apply both criteria to the surface and preview dots. Do not rely only on byte hashes, silently narrow the registry, exempt the lattice, or target rare holes.
- Reuse the template's pre-existing cached feature stack, `evaluate_holdout.py`, and `submission_writer.py`; do not privately fork shared tools. If a shared tool is wrong, fix it once in the shared template if authorized and report the issue. If access is read-only, stop and document the blocker.
- Withhold **whole fault segments** in spatial blocks with a buffer. Build every catalogue-derived feature exclusively from visible faults. Apply the exact known-fault pixel mask, and score pooled DTI with α=0.2, β=0.8 and a 300 m triangular kernel. Test each feature separately for leakage; AUC above 0.90 is leakage until disproved.
- Report performance only as `HOLDOUT-DTI`, including evaluator version, withheld positive count and 95% CI, or as `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED` copied from a receipt. Clearly distinguish a scientific hypothesis, the holdout proxy, and the hidden leaderboard. Do not invent missing results.
- Stop after at most **three experiments or two hours**. Do not use a competition slot for an idea that does not beat the current same-holdout best. A distinct selector must approve promotion and respect the user's weekly cap and the live platform limit. Never submit automatically. [Staff clarification](https://community.drivendata.org/t/submission-amount-question/11553/2) says the competition website's limit prevails over the rules' three-per-week parenthetical.

## Validation, run card and handoff

- Validate the **saved bytes**, not just arrays: one float32 band; exact authenticated sample CRS, shape and transform; no NaN, infinity or nodata in the footprint; finite in-footprint values in [0,1]. Respect the official outside-footprint instruction, but do not assume an untested alternate encoding will be accepted. Explicitly investigate the reported earlier portal error, `Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]`, without inventing its cause. No human inspection is required for validation.
- End a run with one JSON run card specifying hypothesis, mechanism, a named non-fault mimic, holdout DTI/CI and evaluator/count, correlation/overlap against the full registry, raster SHA-256, byte-level validator output, unique submission name and a note no longer than 140 characters, and a `promote` or `negative` verdict. Use `null` for unmeasured fields; never represent a proposed name as a real file. Promotion is separate.
- When justified, provide the exact approved file/name/note and competition instructions. Otherwise give an early, unambiguous **do not submit** status and prioritized unblockers. Research artifacts may be downloadable but must not be mislabeled as authorized submissions.
- Make concrete improvements, complete three passes (implementation/verification; bugs and edge cases/fixes; original-requirements recheck/improvement), and create and merge a PR to main if permissions and checks permit. Preserve data provenance, public-license details, uncertainty, and reproducibility throughout.
