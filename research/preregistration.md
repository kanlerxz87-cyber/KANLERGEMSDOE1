## Frozen protocol

- Only HC3 is the candidate lane; no post-result switching or tuning. Maximum three experiments or two hours, whichever comes first. No competition upload, no slot selected.
- Reuse the shared template's cached features, `evaluate_holdout.py`, and `submission_writer.py`; do not silently fork them. The checkout has none of these files, so their precise pinned source and availability must be verified first.
- Hide whole fault segments, buffer spatially, compute all catalogue-derived features from visible faults alone, and mask visible faults pixel-exactly. Score pooled distance-weighted Tversky (alpha 0.2, beta 0.8, 300 m triangular kernel) and bootstrap spatial blocks. Compare a fold-local same-evaluator current-best control; do not compare different evaluators as a leaderboard forecast.
- Test every feature alone with a two-sided holdout AUC. AUC above 0.90 triggers a leakage investigation, not automatic confidence. Do not use `dist_known_fault_px` or any derived all-catalogue column.
- Check Spearman rank correlation and the directional fraction of candidate dots within three pixels against **every** registered raster, first for a preview before final placement, and then again for final dots. Correlation above 0.90 or overlap above 70% is a duplicate and an immediate stop, with no density/lattice exception invented locally. A fresh hash alone is not novelty.
- Promote only after a positive paired holdout confidence interval versus the best comparable control, all leakage/uniqueness and saved-byte checks pass, and a separate weekly-slot selector approves. The evaluator name, withheld-positive count and 95% CI must accompany every reported HOLDOUT-DTI. An uncalibrated projection is never a score.
