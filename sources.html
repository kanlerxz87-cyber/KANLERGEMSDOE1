Interpretation policy

Official source, project report, and local measurement are different evidence classes.

  • Official source: organizer, government, or standards body. A URL is not proof that the content was retrieved in this session.
  • Project report: useful provenance and hypothesis evidence, but not an organizer receipt or independent scientific validation.
  • Local measurement: must be reproduced against local bytes and include code, inputs, evaluator version, and uncertainty.
  • Score: report only as `HOLDOUT-DTI` with evaluator, withheld positives, and 95% CI, or as `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED` with a saved receipt bound to file hash.
