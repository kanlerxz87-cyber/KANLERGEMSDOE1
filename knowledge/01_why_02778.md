# Why GEMSDOE32 h33-2-b2 was reported as 0.2778

**Evidence class:** USER/OWNER-REPORT; no organizer submission-page receipt binds that score to the pinned file. This is not a predicted score.

The [reproducible support audit](../evidence/protocol_audit_20261026.json) independently found the following relationships on the pinned mirrored grid. The 121,131-cell H19-5 support contains all 44,090 positive cells of the identical GEMSDOE25 and 30 D2.8-family support; the latter's minimum nearest-neighbor distance is √8 pixels (about 283 m). Applying a Euclidean distance >1 pixel to the mirrored catalogue exactly gives GEMSDOE31 (40,199); >2 pixels gives GEMSDOE32 (37,654). Both XOR mismatch counts are zero. The owner-associated scores 0.2600, 0.2708 and 0.2778 lack authenticated receipts. The support results do not prove the reported score-to-file mapping or causation.

The official [DTI](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) uses best nearby prediction for each hidden truth pixel while charging false-positive mass for every prediction, with α=.2, β=.8 and a 300 m triangular kernel. A sparse, well-spaced map may remove redundant or low-credit mass and improve DTI. But the [organizer masks only exact known-fault pixels](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4), and nearby hidden faults can exist: the voluntary 2-pixel collar can remove useful positives. Causality cannot be established without the private truth and receipts.

Earlier full-map holdout control values are **invalid for promotion** because their placement used all catalogue labels. The v3.0 evaluator additionally omitted false positives in truth-empty cells. Version v3.1 fixes that calculation but no corrected fold-local experiment was run after STOP. The observed rank-1 public board snapshot of 0.4437 on 2026-10-26 is an organizer-published leaderboard observation, not a receipt for h33.
