# Verified geothermal-fault research notes

Reviewed 2026-10-26 UTC. Source metadata and abstracts are accessible via the linked official hosts; the shell network policy here does not permit downloading the actual USGS or GDR products directly.

| Source | Verified observation | Implication / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| [DrivenData task](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) | Target is fault presence; initial scoring uses new expert-labelled faults; final scoring uses an expert-updated label set. | Do not confuse a geothermal vent/favorability map with a fault-trace label. Hidden labels are not available locally. |
| [DrivenData staff on known faults](https://community.drivendata.org/t/scoring-clarification-are-known-usgs-ingenious-faults-masked-when-scoring-and-are-they-in-the-final-round-label-set/11516/4) | Mask is pixel-exact; nearby new faults may count. | A 200 m exclusion is a voluntary model decision, not the organizer's mask; it could remove true discoveries. |
| [USGS GeoDAWN](https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and) | GeoDAWN includes magnetic and radiometric surveys and associated lidar context; the data release is CC0. | Magnetic contrasts can reflect both fault offsets and ordinary lithologic contacts or flight artifacts. |
| [INGENIOUS, GDR 1391](https://gdr.openei.org/submissions/1391) | Public CC-BY regional collection includes seismicity models, geodetic strain, thermal probes, paleo deposits and wells. | Attribution required; mirrors must not be described as organizer-authenticated. Missing observations are not evidence of absence. |
| [USGS blind systems report](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/discovering-blind-geothermal-systems-great-basin-region-integrated-geologic-and) | Southern Gabbs Valley combined gravity, magnetics, temperature, resistivity, chemistry and structure; drilling provided evidence despite no hot springs, fumaroles or paleo deposits. | Surface vents are unnecessary, and apparent geothermal evidence is not by itself a labelled fault. |
| [USGS structural discontinuities](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/structural-discontinuities-and-their-control-hydrothermal-systems-great-basin-usa) | Modeled localization of stress and strain occurs at structural discontinuities including stepovers and terminations. | A basis for hypothesis generation, not an unconditional score predictor. |
| [DOE structural inventory](https://www.osti.gov/dataexplorer/biblio/dataset/1148722) | Categorizes Great Basin systems by structural settings, with blind/surface descriptors. | Selection bias toward previously observed systems; verify license and all site-level provenance before training. |
| [USGS ComCat FDSN API](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/) | Service documentation and [one bounded count query](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/count?format=geojson&starttime=2020-01-01&endtime=2021-01-01&minlatitude=37&maxlatitude=42&minlongitude=-120&maxlongitude=-116&minmagnitude=2) were reachable through the research tool. | A live count demonstrates the API exists, not that the full area/event uncertainties have been downloaded, vetted or shared. |

The two withheld public/private splits are not disclosed by organizers ([staff reply](https://community.drivendata.org/t/how-were-the-new-test-faults-identified-data-sources-and-fault-types/11527/10)). Do not infer their geology from leaderboard fit. Dataset and license suitability remain separate gates.
