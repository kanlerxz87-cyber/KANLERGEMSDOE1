## Why the GEMSDOE32 result is a useful but limited clue

The reported `0.2778` is the highest supplied owner-reported value among these nine sites, not an authenticated score for the pinned artifact. A support audit found a plausible mechanism: a D2.8-spaced ridge family has 44,090 cells; applying strict distance thresholds from the mirrored catalogue reproduces 40,199 and 37,654 supports with zero XOR mismatch for the B=1 and B=2 descendants. Thinning can reduce redundant false-positive mass under the competition's asymmetric DTI, and the B=2 support is consistent with pruning some mapped-line shoulders. Those pixel relationships do not prove the organizer receipt, that this file earned the reported value, or that pruning caused the score change. See [`knowledge/01_why_02778.md`](../knowledge/01_why_02778.md) and the [official competition metric description](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/).

The latest recorded research candidate has `HOLDOUT-DTI = 0.105830` (`GEMSDOE32-HIDE-RECOVER-v3.1`, 22,232 withheld positives, 95% CI `[0.095019, 0.115257]`). This is an internal spatial holdout metric, not a leaderboard projection. Its paired interval versus the fold-local champion reference crosses zero, it does worse on the calibrated SGMC regional benchmark than the cited sibling result, and it fails full-registry uniqueness. Do not infer that it beats a reported `0.2778` result. **Additional reproducibility irregularity:** the OCDA run-card/ablation record describes a 37,654-dot target, but direct rasterio inspection of the archived NaN-outside and zero-outside TIFFs finds 37,649 positive cells in each. The five-pixel mismatch is unresolved; therefore the recorded holdout DTI has not been established for the exact archived TIFF bytes.
