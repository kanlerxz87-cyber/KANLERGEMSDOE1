Official contract

Verified from the problem description, submission format:

  • Single-band GeoTIFF, or a zip containing a single GeoTIFF
  • CRS EPSG:32611 (UTM 11N), 100 m, same bounds as training data
  • float32 values in [0, 1] (this is the error you hit if NaNs or sentinels leak into the grid)
  • Higher = higher confidence of fault presence

This repo’s writer emits a -zeros.tif with nodata=None and 0.0 outside the footprint so every one of the 12,279,160 cells is finite and in [0, 1]. That is the file to upload if a future candidate is authorized. Do not upload the -nan twin to DrivenData.

Portal steps

  1. Log in at DrivenData competition 306.
  2. Open Submit / New submission.
  3. Choose the authorized *-zeros.tif or its single-TIFF .zip.
  4. Note field (optional, ≤140 chars): paste the exact note from the home-page table. Example of a research note, not for use today:
    KANLER H1 IEDR ieq*dil*hgm s3.17 B2 32000d HR=0.0062 | research | DO-NOT-SUBMIT
  5. Submit. If the portal returns Predicted values must be in range [0, 1], you uploaded a file with NaN, Inf, or a nodata sentinel. Use the zeros variant.
