01 / H33 FORENSICS

A useful geometry, not a proven cause.

On the pinned owner mirrors, H33's 37,654 dots exactly equal the 44,090-dot D2.8 raster after removing the 6,436 dots within two pixels of mapped faults. No score-to-file receipt was found. A missing receipt does not prove a later score did not occur.

The official metric gives each true-fault pixel the strongest nearby prediction, while false-positive mass accumulates. Thinning redundant dots or pruning low-yield ones can help—but hidden labels and authenticated receipts are required to establish whether they did. The organizer confirms the known-fault mask is pixel-exact and new faults can lie close to known faults.

02 / REGISTRY CONSTRAINT

A literal rule with a real consequence.

A registered lattice is within three pixels of 5,160,724 of the 5,167,373 cells in the mirrored footprint. Only 6,649 cells are outside its reach. For N distinct candidate dots, at least max(0, N − 6,649) must be near it.

03 / RESEARCH LANE

Test a different physical mechanism.

The proposed HC3 lane tests paired hydrochemical contrasts across coherent magnetic or gravity edges, with sampling-effort controls. It may indicate an under-cover permeable or sealing fault; groundwater mixing at an ordinary lithologic contact is an explicit competing explanation. It is a hypothesis, not a detected fault or projected score.

Our input is a public-data mirror, not independently authenticated official competition data. The required 35-channel shared cache and compliant paired holdout are missing. This audit ran no experiment and generated no TIFF; a separate run produced three research-only files whose holdouts do not meet the required protocol.

04 / DATA & SOFTWARE

Do not confuse a hash with provenance.

Four owner-mirrored files were restored and hash checked; the shared preparation script was run. The mirrored “sample” contains the known-fault mask, while the problem page describes a total-absence example. Separately, the shared pre-placement gate ignores excessive preview overlap, and the shared holdout can omit false positives in zero-truth cells. The shared writer also binarizes probabilities. These issues are reported upstream, not silently patched here.
