    Science and method precedents

    What the literature supports—and what it cannot prove here

    USGS · Great Basin structural controls

    Siler (2023), “Structural discontinuities and their control on hydrothermal systems in the Great Basin, USA”, DOI 10.1144/geoenergy2023-009. The USGS abstract reports localized stress/strain at structural settings hosting hydrothermal systems, particularly stepovers and fault terminations. It motivates a testable physical prior; it does not show that any proposed raster will beat a holdout.

    READ · USGS
    USGS · potential-field analogue

    Glen and Earney, “The influence of pre-existing structures on geothermal springs”. The USGS page describes a Surprise Valley magnetic anomaly and reports springs near local breaks/bends. This is one field analogue, not a universal rule and not a competition holdout result.

    READ · USGS
    DOE GDR · measurement caveat

    Shallow (2-meter) Temperature Surveys in Colorado. Its public record describes measurements intended to investigate upflow structures and explicitly notes that 2-m temperature varies with both solar heating and possible geothermal heating. It is a method precedent, not a GeoDAWN-area input file.

    READ · public GDR
    Peer-reviewed · automated fault mapping

    Mattéo et al. (2021), Journal of Geophysical Research: Solid Earth. The open-access paper evaluates a U-Net for fractures/faults in optical and topographic data. It emphasizes contextual information; its discussion notes that omitting topography can make sub-linear features—including non-tectonic ones—look fault-like. It is methodological context, not a training recipe for this project.

    READ · peer-reviewed
    USGS · lidar mapping / false positives

    USGS high-resolution 3DEP fault-mapping data release (DOI 10.5066/P13NFGLT) demonstrates detailed scarp mapping from 1 m/pixel DEM derivatives with selected GPS validation and attributes for fault confidence and anthropogenic modification. This example is outside the GEMS study area and is a method precedent only. The USGS 3DEP program page says its DEM/lidar products are free and without use restrictions; exact study-area coverage still needs checking.

    Evidence labels

    What counts as a score in this project?

    HOLDOUT-DTI

    Local hide-and-recover measurement

    Must include the evaluator version, number of withheld positives, and 95% CI. It is a research measurement, not an organizer or leaderboard score.

    ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED

    Receipt-bound result

    Use only when copied from the matching organizer submission-page receipt. The receipt should identify the exact submission; the filename alone is insufficient.

    OWNER-REPORTED

    Historical site claim

    Useful for finding artifacts and building hypotheses, but not independently confirmed by this checkout. Keep it labeled unverified and never use it as a calibrated prediction.

    Audit boundary: these sources were read, but this checkout has not downloaded or hash-verified any competition GeoTIFF or candidate raster, has not replayed a holdout, and has not uploaded to DrivenData. Statements about those events are intentionally absent.
