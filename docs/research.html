Why was 0.2778 reported?

Evidence class: USER/OWNER-REPORT for 0.2778 (no submission-page receipt binds it to exact bytes here). What follows separates byte-level fact, metric algebra, and this session's new fold-local test — the three must not be conflated.

Byte-level fact: one nested family

The 121,131-cell H19-5 support contains the 44,090-cell D2.8 support (minimum spacing √8 px ≈ 283 m; GEMSDOE25 and GEMSDOE30 supports are identical). Deleting D2.8 cells within 1 px of the catalogue gives GEMSDOE31 exactly (40,199); within 2 px gives GEMSDOE32 exactly (37,654); XOR mismatches are zero (audit JSON). Reported scores rise monotonically as the family sparsifies: 0.1922 → 0.2600 → 0.2708 → 0.2778 (all owner reports).

Metric algebra: why sparsification pays

For binary dots the official DTI (α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel) is T/(0.2·(T+n−M)+0.8·|G|), where T is kernel credit summed over truth pixels and M self-credit over dots. Truth credit saturates at 1 per truth pixel (max-kernel), so neighbouring dots around one fault pixel add denominator without numerator. Dotting at √8 px spaces the 300 m disks edge-to-edge — an efficient covering code — and collar pruning then deletes halo dots whose expected credit is below the marginal bar (≈0.2·DTI for a non-redundant dot). The large reported jump is therefore an emission-geometry effect on a fixed ranking, not new geology.

New fold-local evidence (2026-10-27)

Rebuilding the champion method honestly — visible-only B2 collar inside each hide-and-recover fold — gives HOLDOUT-DTI 0.1002 [0.0869, 0.1138] (v3.1, 22,232 withheld positives). Two pre-registered attempts to improve on it failed with paired CIs excluding zero: a new 5-family concordance ranking (0.0518) and a gated relaxation of the collar (0.0942). The collar helps on withheld catalogue segments because near-trace halos are mostly false-positive mass — while organizer staff confirm genuine new truth can sit within 300 m of known traces (splays/corrections are an explicit aim), so the collar remains a bet whose live value only receipts can settle.

What would actually beat it

At DTI≈0.28 the marginal bar for one more good dot is low (≈0.056 kernel credit); gains must come from a better ranking of corridors at fixed budget. The public leader at 0.4544 (ORGANIZER-PUBLISHED, 2026-10-27) and the fleet's reported 0.3195 weighted hybrid both point the same way: consensus/learned ranking quality, not thinner dotting. This session's fixed-weight concordance was not that ranking (withheld AUC 0.542; the road veto alone cost ≈0.026 AUC). The pre-registered successor is a spatially-blocked PU learner over line features — see the research slate.
