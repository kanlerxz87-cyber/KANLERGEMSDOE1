Why EXP-2 Failed (`0.025006`) and Why EXP-3 Won (`0.105830`): Positive-Unlabeled Split Inversion

Under Protocol Rule 5, negative results are first-class scientific deliverables:

  1. The EXP-2 Trap: Fold-local continuation fields (`fold_vis_tip_continuation` and `fold_vis_stepover_relay_bridge`) are strictly zeroed on `visible_mask` so they highlight unmapped forward extensions (`& ~vis`). When fed directly into a Positive-Unlabeled tree trained with `y = visible_mask`, 100% of positive training pixels (`y = 1`) have value `0.0` while unlabeled pixels (`y = 0`) ahead of fault tips have values `> 0`! The GBDT splits `tip_continuation <= 0 → higher P(y=1)`, actively suppressing fault tips (`HOLDOUT-DTI = 0.025006`).
  2. The EXP-3 Solution: Decoupling Stage-1 geophysical `OCDA` likelihood (trained on 60 purely geophysical channels without binary coverage flags) from Stage-2 logistic calibration fit strictly on 20% inner-withheld training-quadrant fault segments learns positive structural weights (`w_tip ≈ +2.47, w_relay ≈ +0.45`) and raises `GEMSDOE32-HIDE-RECOVER-v3.1` to `0.105830` (`+27.3%` in SW Walker Lane).
