2. Key Theorem Discovered in EXP-2 → EXP-3: Positive-Unlabeled Split Inversion

In EXP-2, fold-local structural continuation features (fold_vis_tip_continuation, single-feature withheld AUC = 0.7295; fold_vis_stepover_relay_bridge, withheld AUC = 0.7386) were zeroed on visible_mask and passed directly into HistGradientBoostingClassifier trained with y = visible_mask. Because 100% of training positives had continuation == 0 while distant training negatives (d_vis > 4 px) had continuation > 0, the tree inverted the physical sign (0.104042 → 0.025006).

EXP-3 Resolution: Decoupling Stage-1 geophysical OCDA probability from a Stage-2 logistic combiner calibrated strictly on 20% inner-withheld training-quadrant fault segments restored strictly positive weights across all 8 folds (w_tip ≈ +2.47, w_relay ≈ +0.45), lifting overall HIDE-RECOVER-v3.1 to 0.105830 and SW Walker Lane DTI by +27.3% (0.0665 → 0.0846).
