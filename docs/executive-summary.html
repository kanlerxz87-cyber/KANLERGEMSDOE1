Why GEMSDOE32 h33-2-b2 reported 0.2778 — and why it cannot be copied

The DTI (α=0.2, β=0.8, 300 m triangular kernel) gives each truth pixel its best nearby prediction credit and charges every predicted pixel for false-positive mass. The H19-5 dotted support (121,131 px) contains the D2.8 family (44,090 px); thinning by catalogue distance — B=1 gives GEMSDOE31 (40,199 px, 0.2708), B=2 gives GEMSDOE32 h33-2-b2 (37,654 px, 0.2778) — removes redundant, closely-spaced dots that add little truth credit but still incur FP mass. Reported DTI rises with sparsity (0.1922 → 0.2600 → 0.2708 → 0.2778). The organizer masks only exact known-fault pixels, so nearby hidden truth can still earn credit.

This is a geometric hypothesis about pixel support, not proof of what caused the private leaderboard result: no score-to-file receipt exists, the organizer's test-fault sources are undisclosed, and the earlier full-map holdouts leaked catalogue information. Copying h33-2-b2 is additionally a duplicate (97.58% overlap with GEMSDOE36, identical lineage to GEMSDOE25/30) and fails the literal registry rule.
