What the reported GEMSDOE32 result may indicate

The repository's historical raster audit describes the GEMSDOE32 reference as a thinned and collar-pruned member of a family derived from H19-5. A plausible scoring mechanism is reduced redundancy among nearby ridge pixels and fewer positives close to the mapped-fault mask, while the distance-weighted metric can reward nearby predictions. This is a forensic hypothesis, not a demonstrated causal effect: the reported 0.2778 is USER/OWNER-REPORT, not ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED, and no controlled pair of organizer submissions isolates the transformation.

The local hide-and-recover benchmark is a different evaluation target from the organizer leaderboard. A holdout value is not a leaderboard estimate. No calibrated, in-range, validated conversion is available; therefore this project reports the organizer score estimate as unavailable, not a projection.
