When a future file is approved

  1. Confirm the decision and quota

    The candidate must pass both novelty stages, individual feature canaries, a paired spatial holdout, and the saved-byte format checks. A separate selector must approve a slot within the user’s cap. The remaining quota is not available here; staff say the competition website limit prevails over the rules’ three-per-week parenthetical.

  2. Download the exact approved TIFF

    Use the link and SHA-256 from that future decision. Do not rename an old TIFF or submit a PDF, audit JSON, or screenshot. If a download is packaged as a ZIP, extract the GeoTIFF; the official format specifies a GeoTIFF, not a ZIP upload.

  3. Check the official format

    The written specification calls for one float32 band, EPSG:32611, matching bounds and 100 m grid, values in [0,1], and null/NaN outside the bounds. The exact shape and transform must match an authenticated competition template. Local validation does not prove portal acceptance.

  4. Use the competition form

    Enroll and sign in on DrivenData, open the competition’s Submit page, choose the approved GeoTIFF, and enter its short name and note where requested. Check your weekly limit on that page before proceeding.

  5. Save the receipt

    Record the file SHA-256, name, note, date and organizer score from the submission page. Only then can a result be attributed to those exact bytes. Choose one final entry for both prize rounds.

TROUBLESHOOTING

“Predicted values must be in range [0, 1]”

Non-finite or out-of-range values inside the footprint, including floating-point nodata sentinels, fail local checks. The specific cause of the reported portal error is unverified. Do not assume zero outside is accepted: the written specification asks for null/NaN outside. Confirm the exact template and organizer guidance before trying again.

REQUIRED CHECKS

  • 01 Assigned method lane
  • 02 Every-feature leakage canary
  • 03 Paired spatial holdout + CI
  • 04 Surface and final-dot novelty
  • 05 Exact-file format validation
  • 06 Separate quota-aware selector

No human geological inspection is used as a substitute for these automated checks.
